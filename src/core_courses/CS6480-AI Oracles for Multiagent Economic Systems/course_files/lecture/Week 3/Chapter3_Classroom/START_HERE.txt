MODEL BOUNDARY
Four advertisers, three positions, unweighted GSP, no reserve or budgets.
Values (10,8,6,4), environment CTRs (0.10,0.06,0.03).
All players may bid any integer from 0 to 12. Ties follow A,B,C,D.
Each unilateral scan fixes every other bid. Equilibrium verdicts concern this finite game.
The lab does not compute FLNE or prove convergence, uniqueness, or general welfare optimality.
